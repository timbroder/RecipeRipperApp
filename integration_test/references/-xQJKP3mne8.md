## Ingredients
- 2 lb chicken breast
- 1 packet ranch seasoning
- smoked paprika to taste
- 10 slice beef bacon
- 30 g green onion
- 500 g cooked rice
- 40 g hot honey
- 150 g Greek yogurt
- 60 g light mayo
- 20 g egg whites
- 25 g jalapeno
- buffalo ranch seasoning to taste
- onion powder to taste
- garlic powder to taste
- lime juice to taste

## Nutrition
- **Calories:** 530
- **Protein:** 56g
- **Fat:** 14g
- **Carbs:** 37g
- **Servings:** 5

## Directions
1. Coat the chicken breast with ranch seasoning and smoked paprika.
2. Cook the chicken on a pan for three to five minutes on each side until done.
3. Set the cooked chicken aside.
4. Chop up the beef bacon and add it to the pan.
5. Add green onion to the pan.
6. Stir in the cooked rice and hot honey.
7. In a bowl, mix Greek yogurt, light mayo, egg whites, jalapeno, buffalo ranch seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, and lime juice.
8. In five bowls, layer the chicken, rice, and top with the sauce.
